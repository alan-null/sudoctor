About the project

About sudoctor

System health checks, findings, and report history for your Linux hosts.

From checks to a clearer picture

sudoctor collects health reports for your hosts. This dashboard brings their latest results and previous snapshots together, so you can review findings and see how resource usage changes over time.

Reading your reports

Overview summarizes host health. Open a host to explore its history, select a snapshot, and inspect operating system, update, application, and service checks. Available checks depend on the report collected from that host.

Healthy means no issues were reported. Warnings need attention, critical findings indicate a problem, and unknown means a result is unavailable. Report timestamps help you judge how current the results are.

Your data, or a quick look around

Use Open reports to load JSON reports, or Refresh to reload reports from your server. View demo loads three fictional hosts with four snapshots each. Demo reports are labeled; on local and Docker dashboards, Refresh returns to your real reports.
